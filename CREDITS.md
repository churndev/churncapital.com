# Image credits

Every wallpaper on the homepage comes from one of the photographs below. All of them are public domain. The homepage does not show attribution, because public domain does not require it. This file is the license record and is not part of the served site.

## What changed

Each master was scaled with Lanczos to 1280, 1920, 2560, and 3840 pixels wide and encoded as AVIF (SVT-AV1 CRF 36, preset 8) and WebP (quality 75). Nothing was cropped, recolored, captioned, or otherwise edited. No width is an upscale: every master is at least 3840 pixels wide.

Output paths are `public/images/<theme>/<id>-<width>.avif` and `.webp`.

Where the note says "Commons 3840 JPEG rendition," the master was Wikimedia Commons' JPEG of the public-domain TIFF at 3840 pixels wide. The four output widths were encoded from that JPEG. The TIFF itself is not in the repo.
